About Sangram Sevabhavi Sanstha

"संग्राम सेवाभावी ही सामाजिक संधीला घेऊन काम करणारी संस्था आहे."

"संस्थेची स्थापना 19 जून 1995 रोजी प्रल्हादराव पालवे (तात्या) यांनी केली."

"सदर संस्था Maharastra Public Trust Act 1950 अंतर्गत नोंदणीकृत आहे."

Sangram sevabhavi sanstha, pimpalner is a charitable organisation working for educational, social & community devolopement of disadvantaged and underserved section of the society. The organization is committed to creating opportunities for education, degnity, social devolopement and a better future.

Our Heritage

Institutional Timeline

19 June 1995

Foundation by Late Pralhad Baburao Palve (तात्या)

The organization was instituted with a resolute vision to serve disadvantaged rural families, ensuring educational access and dignity for every child.
